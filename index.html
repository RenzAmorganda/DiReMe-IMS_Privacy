About DiReMe IMS

DiReMe IMS is an Intelligent Inventory Management System built specifically for motor shops and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs). It streamlines day-to-day retail and warehouse operations by combining real-time stock tracking, point-of-sale (POS) processing, and on-device AI sales forecasting inside a secure Android application.

Designed with strict Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) principles, every action and access request is verified to keep branch operations isolated, auditable, and resilient.

Key Features & Capabilities

DiReMe IMS equips businesses with essential tools to manage inventory accurately and efficiently:

  • Real-Time Inventory Tracking — Monitor product names, SKUs, prices, and stock quantities across branches
  • Point-of-Sale (POS) Processing — Record sales transactions, item quantities, and totals seamlessly
  • Fast Barcode Scanning — Integrated ZXing camera scanning for rapid product lookup and checkout
  • On-Device AI Sales Forecasting — Powered by TensorFlow Lite to generate branch sales predictions directly on your device
  • Role & Branch Isolation — Tailored permissions for Managers, Cashiers, and Stock Persons

Zero Trust Security Architecture

Every request in DiReMe IMS is authenticated, authorized, and continuously validated:

Core Security Highlights
  • Firebase Authentication with encrypted credential storage
  • Email OTP two-factor authentication required on every login
  • reCAPTCHA v2 verification during account registration
  • Step-up password re-authentication prior to sensitive operations
  • Granular Firestore security rules enforcing role and branch-level isolation
  • Comprehensive timestamped audit logs for compliance and accountability
